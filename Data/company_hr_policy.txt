NovaTech Solutions Employee Handbook HR Policy Version 3.2 January 2026

== Leave Policy ==
Employees are entitled to 24 days of paid annual leaves per calendar year. Sick leaves are capped at 10 days per year. Any additional leave required for medical emergencies must be approved by the HR department and the immediate reporting manager.

== Work From Home Policy ==
All employees who have completed their probation period of 6 months are eligible for work from home arrangements. Employees in their probation period may request work from home only in exceptional circumstances with manager approval. Regular work from home requests require a minimum of 48 hours advance notice.

== Expense Reimbursement ==
Employees can claim reimbursements for travel, internet, and office setup expenses up to $500 per year. Original receipts must be submitted via the internal HR portal within 30 days of the expense.

== Probation and Confirmation ==
During the probation period, all new employees undergo a six-month evaluation period. Upon successful completion of the probation period, employees will receive a confirmation letter and become eligible for additional company benefits.

== Performance and Management ==
Performance reviews are conducted bi-annually. The management evaluates employees based on project deliverables, team collaboration, and adherence to company values.

== Code of Conduct ==
NovaTech Solutions enforces a strict zero-tolerance policy towards harassment or discrimination of any kind. All employees are expected to maintain professional behavior at all times.
